The editable derived database is distributed in this app as
country-boundaries-detailed.json. It contains land contours for AND, GGY, GIB,
IMN, JEY, LIE, MCO, MLT, SMR, VAT, extracted and simplified to 5 metre tolerance
from OpenStreetMap data downloaded 2026-11-21 via Geofabrik and the OSM API.
Administrative waters are excluded. Other country outlines in
country-boundaries.json are Natural Earth public-domain data.
